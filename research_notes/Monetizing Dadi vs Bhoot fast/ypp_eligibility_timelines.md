# YPP Eligibility Tiers, Thresholds and Realistic Timelines for a New Indian (Hindi) Channel — as of 24 Nov 2026

> Method note: WebFetch could not reach support.google.com, tubefilter.com or ppc.land from this session (host not allowed in network settings), so the findings below come from search-result extracts (titles and snippets) of those pages and of other outlets. Numbers that appear consistently across official Google pages and several outlets are treated as confirmed. Anything from a single secondary source is flagged.

## 1. Current YPP thresholds, and the change already announced for 1 Feb 2027

### Takeaway
Until **31 Jan 2027**, full ad-revenue YPP requires **1,000 subscribers + either 4,000 qualified public watch hours (last 12 months) or 10M qualified public Shorts views (last 90 days)**. On **10–11 Aug 2026** YouTube announced that from **1 Feb 2027** new applicants will need **1,000 subs + 8,000 qualified watch hours (365 days) or 20M qualified Shorts views (90 days)**. The lower "expanded YPP" fan-funding tier (**500 subs + 3 public uploads in 90 days + 3,000 watch hours/12 months or 3M Shorts views/90 days**) is reported as **unchanged**, and Google's help page lists India as eligible. A channel starting from 0 in the last week of Nov 2026 will almost certainly be judged against the **doubled 2027 bar** for ad revenue, because reaching even the current 4,000 hours or 10M Shorts views in about 9 weeks is very unlikely.

### Cited Findings
- Current official thresholds: 1,000 subscribers with 4,000 qualified watch hours in the last 12 months, or 1,000 subscribers with 10M qualified Shorts views in the last 90 days. 2-Step Verification is required. — [YouTube Help: YPP overview & eligibility](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72851?rd=1) (search extract, page current Nov 2026)
- Announced 10 Aug 2026, effective **1 Feb 2027**: new applicants for ads and Premium revenue need 1,000 subs plus 8,000 qualified watch hours in the previous 365 days **or** 20M qualified Shorts views in 90 days. Existing partners are grandfathered and must accept updated modules by 31 Jan 2027. Described as the first significant change to YPP eligibility since 2018. — [Tubefilter, 10 Aug 2026](https://www.tubefilter.com/2026/08/10/youtube-partner-program-ad-eligibility-requirements-shorts/); [NewscastStudio, 10 Aug 2026](https://www.newscaststudio.com/2026/08/10/youtube-partner-program-monetization-changes-2027/); [vidIQ blog](https://vidiq.com/blog/post/youtube-partner-program-changes-2027/); [Digital Trends](https://www.digitaltrends.com/movies/youtube-just-doubled-the-grind-before-creators-can-start-making-ad-money/)

### Inferences
- For a channel launched around 24–30 Nov 2026, the window to apply under the old 4,000h/10M rules closes 31 Jan 2027 (about 9 weeks). Hitting 1,000 subs + 4,000 hours or 10M Shorts views in 9 weeks from zero would take a viral outlier. Plan for the 8,000h/20M bar.
- The 500-sub expanded tier is the realistic first milestone. It pays only through fan funding and Shopping, not ads.

### Gaps

## 2. What each tier unlocks, and what is unavailable before YPP

### Takeaway
The **expanded tier (500 subs)** unlocks fan funding (channel memberships, Super Chat, Super Stickers, Super Thanks) and Shopping, but **no ad revenue**. The **full tier (1,000 subs + hours/views)** adds Watch Page ad revenue, YouTube Premium revenue and Shorts Feed revenue sharing. From 1 Feb 2027, Shorts revenue also needs a rolling **10M qualified Shorts views per 90 days**, checked every month. Before YPP, the creator earns **nothing** from YouTube, even if ads appear on the videos.

### Cited Findings
- Expanded YPP unlocks fan funding (memberships, Super Chat, Super Stickers, Super Thanks) and Shopping. Ad revenue sharing requires the full 1,000-sub threshold. — [YouTube Help: expanded YPP](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/13429240); [vidIQ: Monetize with only 500 subscribers](https://vidiq.com/es/blog/post/youtube-relaxes-monetization-rules/)
- YouTube Shopping affiliate in India launched with Flipkart in Oct 2024. Later reporting says the affiliate programme was extended to creators with 500 subscribers (date not confirmed). — [TechCrunch, 24 Oct 2024](https://techcrunch.com/2024/10/24/youtube-brings-its-affilate-program-to-india-partners-with-walmart-owned-flipkart); [Deccan Herald](https://www.deccanherald.com/technology/youtube-shopping-affiliate-programme-expands-to-creators-with-500-subscribers-3950747)
- From 1 Feb 2027, even existing partners need ≥10M qualified Shorts views in a rolling 90 days to get Shorts ad and Premium revenue for the next month. Falling below it pauses only Shorts revenue: long-form earnings continue and the channel stays in YPP. YouTube VP (Hanif) quoted: "In order to earn Shorts Rev Share in the upcoming month, you'll have to have had 10 million views over the last 90 days." — [ppc.land](https://ppc.land/youtube-cuts-shorts-pay-for-channels-under-10m-views-from-february-2027/); [ppc.land: VP cites "a few cents"](https://ppc.land/youtube-vp-cites-a-few-cents-to-justify-10-million-shorts-view-floor/); [quasa.io](https://quasa.io/media/ten-million-shorts-views-becomes-a-monthly-gate-not-just-an-entry-target); [Cartoon Brew](https://cartoonbrew.com/shorts/youtube-shorts-monetization-rule-changes-265682.html); [Tech Times, 11 Aug 2026](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/323954/20260811/youtube-partner-program-doubles-entry-bar-adds-monthly-shorts-earnings-gate.htm)
- Ads appearing on a non-member's videos do not mean the channel is in YPP. Such a channel gets no share of that revenue. — [YouTube Help: YPP overview](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72851?hl=en-GB) (search extract)
- Commentators note YouTube can run ads on Shorts without paying creators who miss the threshold. — [Kotaku](https://kotaku.com/youtube-partner-program-monetization-change-youtube-shorts-ad-revenue-2000723397)

### Inferences
- A Shorts-heavy Hindi channel faces two hurdles from Feb 2027: a 20M-view entry route, and a 10M/90-day floor to keep Shorts revenue. A long-form "Dadi vs Bhoot" episode format may be the more dependable route to ad revenue.

### Gaps
- Whether Super Thanks and memberships in India have extra eligibility checks (age 18+, etc.) was not verified this session.

## 3. What counts as qualified ("valid public") views and hours

### Takeaway
**Shorts-feed watch time does NOT count** toward the 4,000/8,000 watch hours. Only public long-form videos (podcasts included) and archived livestreams count. Private, unlisted and deleted videos are excluded, as are views served as ads. Shorts views must be public, *engaged* views in the Shorts feed. Loops and image posts don't count.

### Cited Findings
- Qualified watch hours come from public long-form videos (including podcasts) or archived livestreams. Excluded: private, unlisted and deleted videos, videos watched as an ad, and Shorts watch hours. — [blog.youtube (Aug 2026)](https://blog.youtube/news-and-events/youtube-monetization-qualified-watch-hours-shorts-views/)
- Qualified Shorts views are public, engaged views in the Shorts feed. Private, unlisted or deleted Shorts, ad-served Shorts and image posts don't count, and loops aren't engaged views. — [blog.youtube](https://blog.youtube/news-and-events/youtube-monetization-qualified-watch-hours-shorts-views/); [ppc.land: YouTube clarifies eligibility metrics](https://ppc.land/youtube-clarifies-partner-program-eligibility-metrics-for-watch-hours/)
- "Public watch hours from Shorts views in the Shorts Feed won't count towards the 4,000 public watch hours threshold." — [YouTube Help: YPP overview](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72851?hl=en-GB) (search extract)
- One analysis notes YouTube has only described a label change ("valid public" to "qualified"). It has not said whether the counting logic itself changes. — [search summary of Android Authority / 9to5Google coverage](https://androidauthority.com/youtube-partner-program-requirements-3696695) (flag: uncertain)

### Inferences
- If a long video is watched inside the Shorts feed (as a clip or remix), that time likely does not count. Only Watch Page time on the public long-form video does. This was not confirmed explicitly.

### Gaps
- No official statement found on whether watch time from long videos surfaced inside the Shorts feed counts. Treat it as not counting.

## 4. Application and review process

### Takeaway
Apply via YouTube Studio > Earn > Apply. Accept the base terms, link an AdSense for YouTube account, and have 2-Step Verification on. Officially, review "typically takes about a month", though many creators report faster outcomes. If rejected, the rule commonly reported is a 30-day wait to reapply, rising to 90 days after repeat rejections. That rule comes from secondary sources only. **Even a channel that met thresholds today could not reliably be approved by 30 Nov 2026.**

### Cited Findings
- Steps: Studio > Earn > Apply now > accept base terms > set up/link AdSense for YouTube > "Be reviewed" shows "In progress". 2-Step Verification must be on for the Google Account. — [YouTube Help: YPP overview](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72851?hl=en-gb) (search extract); [vidIQ: get monetized](https://vidiq.com/blog/post/get-monetized-youtube/)
- Review "typically takes about a month", and delays are possible. — YouTube creator tools page via [YouTube Help / vidIQ search summary](https://vidiq.com/pt/blog/post/get-monetized-youtube). Many creators report approval within a week. — [vidIQ](https://vidiq.com/blog/post/get-monetized-youtube/) (anecdotal)
- Borderline channels may need multiple human reviews and take longer. — [VeeFly blog](https://blog.veefly.com/guide/ypp-application-still-under-review) (vendor blog, low reliability)
- Reapply after 30 days if rejected. The wait reportedly extends to 90 days after repeat rejection or suspension. — [VeeFly blog](https://blog.veefly.com/?p=14175) (vendor blog. The two VeeFly posts are inconsistent, and the official page could not be opened)
- Channels must have no active Community Guidelines strikes and must comply with YouTube channel monetization policies. — [YouTube Help: YPP overview](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72851?rd=1) (standard requirement; exact wording not retrieved this session)

### Inferences
- Shortest realistic path, best case: thresholds met, then apply, then about 1–4 weeks of review. Payment then follows AdSense cycles: India payout threshold and monthly payment, plus PAN/tax info. I did not verify these this session.

### Gaps
- No 2026 data found on current median review times. The 30/90-day reapplication wait is not confirmed from an official page this session.

## 5. Realistic growth benchmarks and Indian RPM expectations

### Takeaway
Most new channels take **many months to years** to hit 1,000 subs. vidIQ (Sept 2026, 32.9M channels) found only **~31%** of small channels that uploaded **52+ videos in a year** reached 1,000 subs. A separate vidIQ sample puts the **median time to 1,000 subs at about 16 months**. For Indian audiences, RPM estimates (third-party, unverified) are roughly **₹60–100 per 1,000 views for long-form entertainment** and about **₹2–20 per 1,000 for Shorts**. Even a successful early channel earns modest sums.

### Cited Findings
- vidIQ Research (updated 11 Sep 2026), 32.9M small channels over 360 days: 30.77% of channels with 52+ additional videos reached 1,000 subs, vs 0.20% of channels with no new uploads. Of those tracked, 500,409 reached 1,000 subs. — [vidIQ Research, Sept 2026](https://vidiq.com/research/youtube-uploads-1000-subscribers/)
- Median time to 1,000 subs was about 16 months, from a vidIQ sample of 9,414 channels created after 2023. — [vidIQ blog](https://vidiq.com/blog/post/how-many-videos-to-get-1000-subscribers) (as summarised in search, not the 32.9M study)
- Channels created in 2025 that were ≥95% long-form hit 1,000 subs at a median of 46 uploads. Channels ≥95% Shorts took a median of 90 uploads. — [Skool summary of vidIQ analysis](https://www.skool.com/inbound-on-youtube/how-many-videos-does-1000-subscribers-actually-take) (secondary, unverified)
- vidIQ estimates most creators reach 4,000 watch hours within 6–12 months of consistent uploading. — [vidIQ: 4,000 hours](https://vidiq.com/blog/post/how-to-get-4000-hours-watch-time-on-youtube/). Other blogs say about 12 months to 1,000 subs and about 2 years to 4,000 hours. — [thedonutwhole](https://www.thedonutwhole.com/how-long-does-it-take-to-reach-4000-watch-hours/) (low reliability)
- Other vidIQ 2026 benchmarks: [subscriber benchmarks, July 2026](https://vidiq.com/research/youtube-subscriber-benchmarks-2026/); [upload frequency study, July 2026](https://vidiq.com/research/youtube-upload-frequency-study/)
- India RPM estimates (third-party): entertainment/vlog long-form about ₹60 RPM for a mid-sized channel, and about ₹80 at 5 lakh monthly views. — [Upgrowth: YouTube CPM India 2026](https://upgrowth.in/youtube-cpm-india-guide-2026/); [Upgrowth](https://upgrowth.in/youtube-cpm-india/). India Shorts RPM: tech ₹4–20, gaming ₹2–8, education ₹3–12. — [Fluxnote: Shorts RPM India 2026](https://fluxnote.io/guides/youtube-shorts-rpm-india-2026). Shorts earn about one-tenth of long-form per 1,000 views on the same channel. — [Fluxnote: per 1,000 views India](https://fluxnote.io/guides/how-much-youtube-pays-per-1000-views-india). Seasonal: December CPMs reportedly 40–60% above the annual average. — same Fluxnote/Upgrowth guides (unverified)
- vidIQ Hinglish guide for Indian creators (2026). — [vidIQ: YouTube par paise kaise milte hain](https://vidiq.com/blog/post/Youtube-par-paise-kaise-milte-hain-india-guide)

### Inferences
- Illustration (estimate only): 1 lakh long-form views at ₹60–100 RPM is about ₹6,000–10,000. 10M Shorts views at about ₹3–10 RPM is about ₹30,000–1,00,000, but Shorts RPMs for new kids/entertainment Hindi channels are likely at the low end.
- A horror-comedy "Dadi vs Bhoot" format may attract kids. If the content is designated "made for kids", personalised ads are off and RPM drops further. I did not research this in this session.

### Gaps

- No India-specific data was found on time-to-1,000 subs.

## 6. Buying views/subs, sub4sub and "monetization shortcuts"

### Takeaway
YouTube's Fake Engagement and Spam policies prohibit buying subscribers, views or watch time, using bots, and sub4sub. Content or channels can be removed or terminated. Purchased subs and views are typically filtered out and don't count toward thresholds. Secondary sources report YPP rejection or removal for channels that use these tactics.

### Cited Findings
- Prohibited: artificially inflating metrics by automated systems or by serving videos to unsuspecting viewers. Also prohibited: offering to subscribe to another creator's channel only if they subscribe back ("sub4sub"). Channels that don't comply "may be terminated". — [YouTube Help: Fake engagement policy](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/3399767?hl=en); [YouTube Help: Spam, deceptive practices & scams](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/2801973?hl=en-GB)
- Spam or purchased subscribers are removed from counts and don't affect views or watch time. — [vidIQ: Does buying subscribers work?](https://vidiq.com/blog/post/free-subscribers-youtube/) (secondary)
- Reported possible removal from YPP, and payments held up to 90 days. — [climbtheladder](https://climbtheladder.com/can-you-buy-subscribers-for-youtube-the-consequences/); [howsociable](https://howsociable.com/can-you-get-banned-for-buying-youtube-subscribers) (low reliability, not confirmed officially)
- Views from YouTube/Google Ads promotion do not count as qualified watch hours or Shorts views. — [blog.youtube](https://blog.youtube/news-and-events/youtube-monetization-qualified-watch-hours-shorts-views/)

### Inferences
- There is no legitimate shortcut. Paid promotion via Google Ads is allowed, but those views don't count toward YPP. Bought or bot engagement risks the channel itself.
- **Verdict for "monetized by Monday 30 Nov 2026":** not possible. A 0-sub channel cannot reach even the 500-sub tier (it needs 3,000 hours or 3M Shorts views as well) in about 6 days, and YPP review alone typically takes up to about a month. Realistic framing: the expanded tier (fan funding) may be reached in months if content hits. Ad revenue from Feb 2027 needs 1,000 subs + 8,000 hours or 20M Shorts views, which for most channels means many months to over a year.

### Gaps
- No official YouTube statement found tying bought engagement specifically to YPP rejection. The official pages only cite removal or termination.
