## 1. Official ranking signals for Reels, and which ones drive non-follower reach

### Takeaway
In January 2025 Mosseri named three top Reels signals: **watch time, likes per reach and sends per reach**. **Sends (DM shares) per reach** matter most for reaching **non-followers** ("unconnected reach"). Likes matter more for reach among existing followers. Instagram has never published numeric weights.

### Cited Findings
- **Jan 2025 (Mosseri video series):** Mosseri named watch time, likes per reach and sends per reach as the three most important ranking signals. — [Social Media Today, "Instagram shares algorithm insights 2025"](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-shares-algorithm-insights-2025/738034/) (host blocked; snippet only); [Keywords Everywhere: confirmed signals](https://keywordseverywhere.com/news/instagram-algorithm-updates/)
- **Watch time:** Instagram weighs both relative watch time (the share of the Reel watched) and absolute watch time (seconds watched). — [Fanpage Karma, 2025](https://www.fanpagekarma.com/insights/instagram-reels-algorithm/) (third-party summary of Mosseri)
- **Sends:** Mosseri said sends predict overall reach better than likes or comments. — [t3n (DE), quoting Mosseri](https://t3n.de/news/reels-instagram-bewerten-mosseri-1744320/)
- **Followers vs non-followers:** Likes matter more for connected reach (followers). Sends matter more for unconnected reach (non-followers). — [onlinemarketing.de](https://onlinemarketing.de/?p=351431) (secondary)
- **Mosseri on sends:** He singled out sends per reach as the metric for growing an audience. He said original creators get more reach than reposters, and that "every post gets a fair chance so a brand new account can break out." — [Uppbeat summary of Mosseri](https://uppbeat.io/blog/creator-news/whats-working-on-instagram-right-now-carousels-and-sends-says-adam-mosseri) (secondary; exact quote and date not verified)
- **Apr 2024:** Instagram changed its ranking to surface more content from smaller, original creators. — [TechCrunch, 30 Apr 2024](https://techcrunch.com/2024/04/30/instagram-is-updating-its-ranking-systems-to-surface-more-content-from-smaller-original-creators)
- **Unverified figure:** The claim that "sends are weighted 3–5x likes for non-follower distribution" comes from third-party "CreatorFlow" data. Instagram has not confirmed it. — [dev.to summary](https://dev.to/stklen/instagram-reels-algorithm-2026-the-3-official-ranking-factors-mme); flagged as unverified in [Keywords Everywhere](https://keywordseverywhere.com/news/instagram-algorithm-updates/), which also warns that many "2026 algorithm update" articles are invented.

### Inferences
- With 0–100 followers, almost all potential reach is unconnected, so **sends per reach** and **watch time (completion and rewatch)** are the levers that matter. Design the Reel so people send it to someone ("tag your dadi", a relatable family joke) and keep it short enough to finish or loop.
- Because signals are measured **per reach**, the first test audience's reaction drives further distribution. A small but strongly engaged first audience beats a large, lukewarm one.

### Gaps
- I found no official 2025–2026 statement ranking saves, comments or follows-from-Reel relative to the top three. Saves and follows are often listed by third parties but are not confirmed as top signals in the snippets I saw.
- The Meta Transparency Center "Instagram Reels AI system card" could not be fetched. Meta's domains were blocked and the card did not appear in search results.

## 2. Originality rules (reposts, watermarks, cross-posting your own content, AI labels)

### Takeaway
Instagram strongly rewards original content. Accounts that mostly repost content without meaningfully changing it lose eligibility for recommendations, and this was extended in **April 2026** from Reels to photos and carousels on a rolling 30-day basis. Logos or watermarks from other apps (such as TikTok) cause a Reel to be left out of recommendations. Your **own** logo is fine. The AI penalty announced in **Aug 2026** targets *unlabelled AI-generated personas or profiles*, not "AI info"-labelled posts in general.

### Cited Findings
- **30 Apr 2026:** Instagram now treats accounts that mostly post unoriginal photos and carousels (not only Reels) as aggregators. Such accounts are ineligible for recommendations, though followers still see their posts. Originality is judged per account over a rolling 30 days. An account becomes eligible again once most of its last 30 days of posts are original. Account Status shows the standing and lets you appeal or remove posts. Licensed or permitted content is exempt. Meme templates and clips count as original only if the edits "enhance" the content. — [TechCrunch, 30 Apr 2026](https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/30/instagram-restricts-reach-of-content-aggregators-in-new-crackdown/); [PetaPixel, 30 Apr 2026](https://petapixel.com/2026/04/30/new-instagram-policies-target-reposted-content/); [Tubefilter, 30 Apr 2026](https://tubefilter.com/2026/04/30/instagram-removes-algorithm-recommendations-repost-content-aggregator); [Instagram Creators blog: Rewarding original creators](https://creators.instagram.com/blog/rewarding-original-creators-on-instagram) (blocked; snippet only)
- **Earlier rule (Reels only, reported as 2024–25):** An account that reposted content it didn't create or meaningfully enhance **10 or more times in 30 days** was removed from recommendation surfaces. — [search summary of TechCrunch, PetaPixel and Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/instagrams-algorithm-overhaul-will-reward-original-content-and-penalize-aggregators-130018977.html) (exact date of the threshold not confirmed)
- **Mosseri on the policy:** He said the penalties target "unoriginal content that [reposters] didn't enhance." — [Tubefilter, 30 Apr 2026](https://tubefilter.com/2026/04/30/instagram-removes-algorithm-recommendations-repost-content-aggregator)
- **Watermarks:** Instagram tries not to recommend Reels that carry logos from other apps. Mosseri clarified that **including your own logo is OK**. — [Social Media Today, "Instagram clarifies including your own logo on a Reel is OK"](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-clarifies-that-including-your-own-logo-on-a-reel-is-ok/730852/) (date ~2024; not verified). The TikTok-watermark penalty dates from Feb 2021. — [Tubefilter, 10 Feb 2021](https://tubefilter.com/2021/02/10/instagram-reels-with-tiktok-watermark-less-discoverable/). The earlier guidance also listed "blurry videos covered with text" and Reels with borders as less likely to be promoted. — [ScreenRant](https://screenrant.com/instagram-reels-tab-tips-for-discovery-no-watermarks/) (2021, outdated but never reported as reversed)
- **AI profiles, 31 Aug 2026:** Instagram renamed its "AI creator" label to **"AI-generated profile"**. Profiles that feature an AI-generated *person* and don't add this label can lose recommendation reach (Explore, Reels). Adding the label voluntarily keeps reach. — [TechCrunch, 31 Aug 2026](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/31/instagram-puts-new-limits-on-undisclosed-ai-profiles/); [Instagram Creators blog](https://creators.instagram.com/blog/ai-generated-profile-label); [Engadget](https://engadget.com/2246914/instagram-will-demote-ai-generated-influencers-if-they-dont-clearly-label-their-account/)
- **"AI info" post label:** This is separate from the profile label. It is applied per post based on C2PA or SynthID metadata. Using AI for editing, captions or graphics does not by itself require the AI-generated profile label. — [Metricool](https://metricool.com/instagrams-new-ai-label/) (third-party). **I found no source saying the "AI info" post label itself reduces reach.**

### Inferences
- An original animated series (own characters, own script, own voice) is clearly "original". Avoid lifted movie clips, other creators' meme footage and unedited templates.
- **Cross-posting your own YouTube Shorts:** No official rule penalises your *own* content. The penalties target (a) other platforms' watermarks and logos and (b) content you didn't create. Export a **clean master file with no YouTube or Shorts UI or watermark** and upload it natively. (This is inferred from the watermark rule. There is no official statement on YouTube Shorts specifically.)
- If the animation uses generative AI: the dadi and bhoot are cartoon characters, not an AI-generated *person* posing as real, so the Aug 2026 profile rule probably doesn't apply. Do not strip AI metadata to avoid labels. Disclosure carries no documented reach penalty, and the policy direction penalises non-disclosure.

### Gaps
- No official statement was found on whether Instagram detects, or down-ranks, content previously posted on YouTube when it has no watermark.
- The full official definition of "original content" and any visible "original content" label could not be verified because the creators blog was blocked.

## 3. Trial Reels (shown to non-followers first)

### Takeaway
Trial Reels launched in Dec 2024 and were expanded in mid-2025 to **public accounts with 1,000+ followers**. Mosseri said Instagram was *considering* lower thresholds. Third-party guides cite **200+ followers for professional accounts**, which is unverified. **A 0–100 follower account most likely cannot use Trial Reels yet.**

### Cited Findings
- **Dec 2024:** Trial Reels launched. They are shown to non-followers first, do not appear on the profile grid or to followers, and come with performance metrics after about 24 hours. You can then share them to followers. — [Meta Newsroom, Dec 2024](https://about.fb.com/news/2024/12/trial-reels-try-content-non-followers/); [TechCrunch, 10 Dec 2024](https://techcrunch.com/2024/12/10/instagram-rolls-out-trial-reels-that-arent-shown-to-a-creators-followers)
- **Jun–Jul 2025:** Mosseri (on Threads) said Trial Reels are available to **all public accounts with at least 1,000 followers**, and that Instagram is considering extending them to smaller accounts. — [BetaNews, 14 Jul 2025](https://betanews.com/2025/07/14/instagram-opens-up-trial-reels-feature-to-more-creators/); [Social Samosa](https://www.socialsamosa.com/news-2/instagram-introduces-wider-access-trial-reels-9493132); [Meta, Jun 2025](https://about.fb.com/news/2025/06/inspiring-creativity-that-brings-people-together/)
- **Conflicting thresholds:** Third-party guides quote the Help Center as **200+ followers for professional accounts and 1,000+ for personal**, with the account required to be eligible for recommendations. Instagram's own text could not be verified. — [Superprofile guide](https://superprofile.bio/guides/instagram-trial-reels) (blocked; snippet); [Planoly](https://www.planoly.com/blog/how-to-post-a-trial-reel-on-instagram); [Hotmart](https://hotmart.com/en/blog/instagram-trial-reels)
- Losing recommendation eligibility also removes access to Trial Reels. — [Planoly, quoting the Help Center](https://www.planoly.com/blog/how-to-post-a-trial-reel-on-instagram)
- Reported limit of **20 Trial Reels per day**. — [search summary of third-party guides](https://posteverywhere.ai/blog/what-are-instagram-trial-reels) (unverified)
- **India context, Sept 2025:** Instagram tested a **Reels-first home screen in India** (and South Korea). This is a separate feature, but it signals heavy Reels distribution in India. — [Meta Newsroom, Sept 2025](https://about.fb.com/news/2025/09/in-india-instagram-debuts-a-reels-first-experience-for-its-mobile-app/); [TechCrunch, 29 Sept 2025](https://techcrunch.com/2025/09/29/instagram-is-testing-a-reels-first-ui-in-india-and-south-korea)

### Inferences
- Today: switch to a **professional (Creator) account**, then check the "Trial" toggle in the Reel composer. If it isn't there, post normally. All followers-first distribution is small anyway at this size, and Instagram still tests new Reels on non-followers.
- Once Trial Reels unlock (200 or 1,000 followers), use them to A/B test hooks for the same episode.

### Gaps
- I could not confirm whether the official Help Center threshold is currently 200 or 1,000 for professional accounts. Check help.instagram.com or the in-app composer.

## 4. Length and format

### Takeaway
Reels can run **up to 3 minutes** and still be recommended to non-followers (Jan 2025). Third-party data conflicts on the best length. Buffer's 2026 dataset shows the highest reach at **90 s–3 min**. Socialinsider shows the highest engagement rate at **45–60 s**, and Reels **over 3 min** do much worse on both. Specs (9:16, 1080×1920, safe zones) are industry convention; I could not open the official spec pages.

### Cited Findings
- **Jan 2025:** Reels were extended from 90 s to **3 min**. Instagram said Reels up to 3 min are eligible to be shown to non-followers in the Reels tab and Explore. — [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-will-recommend-longer-3-minute-reels/737913/); [Deccan Herald](https://www.deccanherald.com/technology/instagram-extends-reels-duration-from-90-seconds-to-3-minutes-3363553); [Instagram creators blog, "Finding success on reels in 2025"](https://creators.instagram.com/blog/the-latest-with-instagram)
- Instagram has **tested 10-minute Reels** but had not rolled them out widely, per the coverage found. Some 2026 blogs claim a 20-minute limit from Dec 2025, which is **unverified**. — [dev.to / search summary](https://dev.to/stklen/instagram-reels-algorithm-2026-the-3-official-ranking-factors-mme)
- **Third-party, Buffer (2.7M Reels, Jan–Aug 2026, published 12 Nov 2026):** 90–179 s Reels got **+7.4% reach** against each account's baseline. Reels of 180 s or more got **−49.3% reach** but slightly higher engagement. — [Buffer](https://buffer.com/library/instagram-reels-length)
- **Third-party, Socialinsider (undated):** 45–60 s Reels had the highest engagement rate (0.35%). Reels over 3 min had 0.15%. — [Socialinsider](https://socialinsider.io/blog/instagram-reels-length/)
- **Third-party:** A cross-platform Shorts study found an 11–20 s "sweet spot". — [Quso.ai](https://quso.ai/research/how-long-are-instagram-reels)
- **Captions and text:** Instagram's own creation guide (as reported) distinguishes text overlays, which highlight key moments, from captions, which make all spoken audio visible. — [Social Media Today, Instagram Reels creation guide](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-shares-reels-creation-tips-in-new-guide/831551/) (blocked; snippet). The "85% watch on mute" figure is a vendor claim. — [Buffer Reels tips 2026](https://buffer.com/resources/instagram-reels-instructions-and-ideas-for-small-businesses/)
- **Safe zones:** Sources conflict. One says keep text and faces out of the top ~14% and bottom ~35%. Another says keep essential text in the middle ~60%. — [Buffer 2026](https://buffer.com/resources/instagram-reels-instructions-and-ideas-for-small-businesses/); [Later](https://later.com/blog/instagram-reels-hacks/) (third-party)
- **Hook:** Front-load the value and put the hook in on-screen text as well as the voiceover. — [Buffer 2026](https://buffer.com/resources/instagram-reels-instructions-and-ideas-for-small-businesses/) (third-party advice, not an official signal)

### Inferences
- For a first Reel from a zero-follower account, a **short, loopable 20–45 s** piece best protects completion and rewatch (watch-time signals). Longer (90 s+) suits an established audience. This is a judgement balancing the conflicting third-party data. Buffer's reach gain for 90 s+ is an average over existing accounts with followers.
- Use 9:16 at 1080×1920. Put a visual gag or confrontation in the first 1–2 s (dadi vs bhoot face-off). Add burned-in Hindi (Devanagari) captions inside the central safe area, and pick a clear cover frame.

### Gaps
- I could not open Instagram's official spec or cover-image pages (blocked).
- I found no official statement on posting frequency for new accounts.

## 5. Audio, voiceover and AI dubbing

### Takeaway
I found no official 2025–2026 statement that original audio or voiceover beats trending audio for reach. Instagram has long told creators to use original audio or its music library, not audio ripped from other apps. **Meta AI Translations** (free, launched 2025) can auto-dub and lip-sync Reels **Hindi ↔ English**, and later other Indian languages. It is available to all public Instagram accounts.

### Cited Findings
- Instagram has said it would prioritise content with original sound or music from the Instagram library. — [search summary of creator tips; Fiverr guide](https://www.fiverr.com/resources/guides/digital-marketing/instagram-reels-tips) (secondary, undated)
- Third parties argue that trending audio gives discovery through the audio page, while original audio builds equity. — [Later](https://later.com/blog/instagram-reels-hacks/) (third-party opinion)
- **Aug 2025:** Meta AI Translations launched for Reels (English ↔ Spanish). It auto-dubs in the creator's own voice and tone, with optional lip sync. — [Meta creators blog](https://creators.facebook.com/blog/meta-ai-translations)
- **Oct 2025:** Hindi and Portuguese were added (EN/ES/HI/PT) on Instagram and Facebook. — [Meta Newsroom, Oct 2025](https://about.fb.com/news/2025/10/translate-dub-and-lip-sync-your-reels-between-hindi-and-english-now-on-instagram-and-facebook/); [TechCrunch, 9 Oct 2025](https://techcrunch.com/2025/10/09/meta-adds-hindi-and-portuguese-support-for-its-ai-translation-feature-for-reels); [Business Today, 10 Oct 2025](https://www.businesstoday.in/technology/news/story/meta-adds-hindi-ai-translation-for-reels-on-instagram-and-facebook-497661-2025-10-10)
- **Nov 2025 / Jan 2026:** Bengali, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada and Marathi were added, along with local fonts. The page date is ambiguous: the URL says 2025/11 and one snippet says 16 Jan 2026. — [Meta Newsroom](https://about.fb.com/news/2025/11/instagram-empowers-creators-to-go-global-with-local-voice-translations-and-fonts/)
- **Eligibility:** All public Instagram accounts, and Facebook creators with 1,000+ followers. Viewers can switch translations off. Up to 2 speakers per Reel according to one outlet, which conflicts with multi-speaker claims elsewhere. — [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/technology/media/instagram-and-facebook-reels-get-hindi-voice-translations-with-meta-ai-1.500302428); [Yahoo/TechRadar](https://tech.yahoo.com/ai/meta-ai/articles/meta-ai-translating-dubbing-reels-020000646.html)

### Inferences
- An original Hindi voiceover with your own music or SFX makes the audio clearly original. A "dadi" catchphrase used as original audio could become a reusable sound, which is upside if others remix it.
- Turning on Meta AI Translations (Hindi → English and other Indian languages) could widen the non-follower pool. Note the lip-sync limits for animated characters, which is untested here.
- Use licensed music from the Instagram library at low volume under the voiceover, never music ripped from other apps or films.

### Gaps
- I found no official data on reach for original vs trending audio, nor on whether a voiceover over library music is classed as "original audio".
- No source says whether AI-dubbed versions are ranked separately or add reach.

## 6. Hashtags, captions/SEO, Hindi support, Collabs, Facebook cross-posting

### Takeaway
**Hashtags were capped at 5 per post or Reel in Dec 2025.** Mosseri says they help with categorisation and search but don't increase reach. Caption keywords matter more: since **10 Jul 2025**, public professional accounts' posts (adult users) can appear in Google and Bing search. Collabs are recommended by third parties. I found no fresh official data on Facebook cross-posting.

### Cited Findings
- **Dec 2025:** Instagram limited hashtags to **5 per post and Reel** (down from 30). Mosseri said hashtags help people find content but do not increase reach, and that generic tags like #reels or #explore don't help. — [Business Today, 19 Dec 2025](https://www.businesstoday.in/technology/news/story/no-more-hashtag-stuffing-instagram-limits-hashtag-usage-to-just-5-per-reel-and-post-507444-2025-12-19); [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-implements-new-limits-on-hashtag-use/808309/); [LatestLY](https://www.latestly.com/technology/instagram-caps-hashtags-at-five-per-post-in-new-policy-shift-amidst-head-of-instagram-mosseris-myth-claims-7245760.html)
- **Third-party, 2021:** Hootsuite's experiment found 3–5 hashtags reached slightly more people than 30 (small sample). — [Hootsuite](https://blog.hootsuite.com/experiment-instagram-hashtags/) (outdated)
- **10 Jul 2025:** Public posts and Reels from professional accounts (users 18+) are eligible for Google and Bing indexing, with an opt-out in settings. Captions are the main searchable text. — [Tubefilter, 18 Jul 2025](https://www.tubefilter.com/2025/07/18/instagram-indexing-update-google-search-results/); [The Week, 12 Jul 2025](https://www.theweek.in/news/sci-tech/2025/07/12/social-media-instagram-posts-and-reels-will-now-pop-up-on-google-and-bing-searches-how-to-enable-disable-this-setting.amp.html)
- Mosseri has said content search on Instagram is "not very good" and that Instagram is "investing more in search". — [Kontentino Q&A](https://www.kontentino.com/q-and-a/instagram-search-seo/) (date conflict: 2025 vs 2026; unverified)
- **Collabs:** Co-posting with similar-sized creators in the same niche is recommended. — [Buffer 2026](https://buffer.com/resources/instagram-reels-instructions-and-ideas-for-small-businesses/) (third-party advice)

### Inferences
- Caption: write a keyword-rich first line in **Hindi plus Hinglish/Roman Hindi** (e.g. "दादी vs भूत | Hindi horror comedy cartoon | funny bhoot story"), then use up to 5 specific hashtags (#hindicomedy #horrorcomedy #animation #dadi #bhoot). This is inferred; Instagram has not published a Hindi-SEO guide.
- Facebook cross-posting of Reels likely adds Facebook reach without hurting Instagram, but this is unverified.

### Gaps
- No official 2025–2026 statement on Facebook cross-posting's effect on Instagram reach.
- No official statement on Hindi or Devanagari keyword search quality in Instagram search.

## 7. What suppresses reach: recommendation guidelines, horror content, account eligibility

### Takeaway
Content that is allowed on Instagram but "may be upsetting, sensitive, violent, graphic/shocking, sexually suggestive, misinformation or spam" is **not recommended** to non-followers. Accounts with repeated violations can become ineligible for recommendations for a while. **Account Status** shows whether you are eligible. Comedic, cartoon horror is not named as restricted, but gore, realistic violence or shock imagery would risk being left out of recommendations.

### Cited Findings
- Content that goes against the Recommendation Guidelines "may be considered upsetting, offensive, or sensitive and we may make it harder to find, rather than removing it." This includes "content that may depict violence, such as people fighting." — [Instagram Help: recommendation guidelines](https://help.instagram.com/313829416281232) (blocked; snippet only)
- Non-recommendable categories reported include violent, graphic/shocking, sexually suggestive, misinformation and spam content. — [TechRadar](https://www.techradar.com/news/instagram-wont-recommend-content-that-vaguely-violates-its-community-guidelines) (older, ~2021)
- Account Status lets professional accounts see samples of posts that conflict with the Recommendation Guidelines and request a review. Accounts that repeatedly post violating content may be ineligible for recommendation for a period of time. — [Instagram Help: Account Status](https://help.instagram.com/539126347315373); [Instagram Creators: recommendations eligibility](https://creators.instagram.com/blog/instagram-recommendations-eligibility-tips-creators) (snippets)
- The guidelines' vague wording has drawn criticism. Artists report getting stuck in "violation loops". — [The Art Newspaper, 1 May 2023](https://www.theartnewspaper.com/2023/05/01/instagrams-new-tools-prove-shadowbanning-is-realand-now-artists-are-trapped)
- Unoriginal or aggregator accounts and unlabelled AI-generated-person profiles also lose recommendation eligibility (see section 2). — [TechCrunch, Apr 2026](https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/30/instagram-restricts-reach-of-content-aggregators-in-new-crackdown/); [TechCrunch, Aug 2026](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/31/instagram-puts-new-limits-on-undisclosed-ai-profiles/)
- The Sensitive Content Control lets viewers choose how much sensitive content they see. — [Instagram Help](https://help.instagram.com/1055538028699165)

### Inferences
- Keep the horror **comedic and cartoonish**: no blood, gore, realistic violence or jump-scare shock imagery in the first frame or cover. Make the comedy obvious from frame 1 so classifiers and viewers read it as comedy, not disturbing content.
- Before posting, check **Settings → Account Status → "Can your content be recommended?"** on the new account.
- Avoid spammy behaviour on a new account, such as mass-following or engagement-bait captions. Instagram lists spam as non-recommendable.

### Gaps
- No official statement was found on horror, scary or paranormal fiction specifically. The guideline text was not fully readable because help.instagram.com was blocked.
- No official data on "low quality" signals beyond the older notes on blurry, low-resolution video and watermarks.
- **Best posting time in India (estimates only, third-party):** 6–9 PM IST appears most often ([WsCubeTech](https://www.wscubetech.com/blog/?p=15209), [Boss Wallah](https://bosswallah.com/blog/creator-hub/best-instagram-reels-posting-time-in-india-boost-views-engagement/)). SocialPilot gives 7:30 AM, 3 PM and 7:30 PM IST on Monday ([SocialPilot](https://www.socialpilot.co/insights/best-time-to-post-reels-on-instagram)). The methods are undisclosed and the data is not from Instagram. Your own Insights will be better once you have followers.
