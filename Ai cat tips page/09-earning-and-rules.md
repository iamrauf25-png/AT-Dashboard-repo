# 09 — Earning plan + rules

| Stage | Start when | What you do |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Free prompt pack | Day 1 | DM it on keyword comments (`05-free-prompt-pack.md`) |
| 2. ₹499 kit | After about 10 reels with steady keyword comments | Notion template + script + video guide + FAQ. Buyers use their own free Notion and Gemini keys. Sell it on a payment page that delivers files automatically. |
| 3. Done-for-you setup | After your first 10 buyers | You set the system up for them at a higher price |
| 4. Affiliate links / brand deals | When engagement is steady | Only tools you really use. Mark every one as an ad or paid partnership. |
| 5. Platform payouts | Long term | Don't plan around them |

## ₹499 kit contents (draft)
1. A duplicate-able Notion template (content calendar + scripts database)
2. The Apps Script file with NICHE/STYLE_LINE placeholders
3. A 10–15 min screen-recorded setup video (Hinglish)
4. FAQ: API key setup, common errors, how to fix a drifting character

## Keep it clean
- Never promise income or results in a reel or on a sales page.
- Gateway fees and any GST come out of the ₹499. Ask a CA about GST and income tax.
- Use Instagram's AI label for AI-generated content. Disclose paid partnerships as required in India.
- Keep brand names (Notion, Gemini) out of your handle and logo.
- Don't copy another creator's mascot, style or handle.
