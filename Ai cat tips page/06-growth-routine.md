# 06 — Growth routine

## Daily (about 45 min). Post time: 8:00 pm IST, same time every day
- [ ] **7:30 pm** Leave 10 genuine comments on posts in the AI and education niche (real sentences, no "nice post 🔥") — 10 min
- [ ] **8:00 pm** Post the day's reel with its P5 cover, the caption from `04`, and the AI label
- [ ] **8:00–9:00 pm** Reply to every comment and send the keyword DMs from `05` — 20 min
- [ ] **Next morning** Check yesterday's reel in Insights and fill in a row of `07-reel-tracker.csv` — 10 min
- [ ] Repost the same video to YouTube Shorts and Facebook Reels

## Weekly (Sunday, 30 min)
| Look at | If… | Then… |
|---|---|---|
| Watch time / drop-off | most viewers leave in the first 3 seconds | rewrite the hook, start with the cat already talking |
| Shares + saves | one topic is clearly highest | make a part 2 of it |
| Keyword comments | low | use a shorter keyword and say it in both clip 1 and clip 5 |
| Follows per reel | views are high but follows low | improve the bio, pinned reels and Start Here highlight |

- [ ] Post one carousel: **Prompt of the Week** (cover + 1 slide prompt + 1 slide example + "Save this")
- [ ] Turn your best comment question into next week's reel ("Answer a comment")
- [ ] DM 2 creators of a similar size about a collab

## After 10 reels
Keep your top 3 topics and hooks and retire the bottom 3. Judge the page after **60 to 90 days** of steady posting.

## Never
Buy followers, join engagement pods, or promise income.
