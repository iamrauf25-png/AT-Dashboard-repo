# voice.md — Mo & Pebble (cartoon channel)

Every yt-* skill reads this file. It was written before the channel had any videos, so it is a
starting point. Rewrite it from the real transcripts once episodes 1–3 are out.

## Who I am talking to

Two people on one couch. An eight-year-old watching on a tablet after school who wants something
to happen every ten seconds, and the parent half-listening beside them who should laugh at least
once per episode without the kid noticing why. The kid is the main audience; never make a joke
the kid has to get for the scene to work.

## How the show actually talks

There is no host on camera. The channel speaks through three voices:

- **MO** — a moth. Fast, sure of himself, always wrong, never sad about it for long. Short
  sentences, lots of exclamation, calls everything "the moon". Talks in plans: "Step one. Step
  two. There is no step three."
- **PEBBLE** — a pebble. Has never moved. Deadpan, one to three words per line, always right.
  The joke is the timing: a beat of silence, then the word. ("Glass." "No." "Light.")
- **NARRATOR** — used only for cold opens and the odd aside. Warm, dry, slightly
  documentary. Talks straight to the viewer ("you").

## Words I never use

"Hey guys", "welcome back", "smash that like button", "don't forget to subscribe" inside an
episode, "epic", "insane", "you won't believe". No channel intro and no title sequence
longer than two seconds.

## Words I do use

Mo: "Pebble. PEBBLE." · "the moon" (for any light) · "Step one." · "I knew it."
Pebble: "No." · "Light." · "Night [number]." (the sign-off line of every episode)

## What I will not claim

- No real-world fact in an episode unless it has a source I can name in the description
  (episode 1's moth-and-light science included).
- No made-up statistics about the channel, views or subscribers.
- No characters, designs or catchphrases borrowed from existing cartoons. Mo and Pebble are
  original and stay that way.

## My format

- Episodes: 2–4 minutes, one location, one problem, two characters. Cold open in the first
  15 seconds, then "EARLIER" rewind or straight into the story.
- Shorts: 20–55 seconds, vertical, one gag, one loop.
- Look: simple 2D, thick outlines, night-time palette (deep blue, warm yellow light).
  Animation is deliberately limited: Pebble never moves, so most of his shots are one
  drawing plus mouth swaps.
- Clean language, no scares, no mean humour. Mo is the butt of the joke, never Pebble.
- Every episode ends on Pebble's sign-off: "Night [number]."
