# Make Dadi worth sending to cousins

A brand-new Instagram account for "दादी vs भूत" has only one realistic route to virality: **Reels built to be sent by DM and watched to the end by strangers.** At zero followers, nearly all reach is non-follower reach. Instagram's head, Adam Mosseri, has said **sends per reach** is the signal that matters most there, alongside **watch time** and **likes per reach**. So the first Reel should be short (about 20–35 seconds), open on a face-off in the first two seconds with burned-in Devanagari text, land a "this is literally my dadi" punchline, and loop. The series is well placed. It is wholly original: own characters, scripts, code-drawn animation and voices. Instagram has spent 2024–2026 cutting recommendations for reposters and rewarding original creators, and has said a brand-new account can break out. The real risks are self-inflicted: film or label music, YouTube watermarks on cross-posts, horror imagery shocking enough to be dropped from recommendations, and jokes about religion, caste or "witch" tropes. Trial Reels, the best testing tool, are almost certainly **not available** below 200–1,000 followers, so the first week is about a clean, consistent launch rather than A/B testing. A methodological warning applies throughout. Instagram's Help Center, its Creators blog, Meta's newsroom and several Indian trade sites were blocked during research. **Most findings come from search-result snippets of the cited pages, not full reads.** Statements marked as inference are the researchers' judgement, not sourced fact.

## Sends and watch time decide whether strangers see a Reel

In January 2025 Mosseri named the three most important Reels ranking signals: **watch time, likes per reach and sends per reach** ([Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-shares-algorithm-insights-2025/738034/); [Keywords Everywhere](https://keywordseverywhere.com/news/instagram-algorithm-updates/)). The split by audience is what matters for a new account. **Likes weigh more for reach among existing followers, while sends weigh more for "unconnected" reach to non-followers** ([onlinemarketing.de](https://onlinemarketing.de/?p=351431); [Social Samosa](https://www.socialsamosa.com/news-2/instagram-reveals-key-factors-for-boosting-reach-8646916)). Mosseri has reportedly said sends predict reach better than likes or comments ([t3n](https://t3n.de/news/reels-instagram-bewerten-mosseri-1744320/)). He has also called sends per reach "probably one of the most important metrics a new creator could track" and said every post "gets a fair chance so a brand new account can break out" ([Uppbeat](https://uppbeat.io/blog/creator-news/whats-working-on-instagram-right-now-carousels-and-sends-says-adam-mosseri)). The exact wording of that quote was not verified. Watch time counts both as the share of the Reel watched and as absolute seconds watched ([Fanpage Karma](https://www.fanpagekarma.com/insights/instagram-reels-algorithm/)), and Mosseri has called time spent watching the biggest signal ([Social Samosa](https://www.socialsamosa.com/news-2/instagram-reveals-key-factors-for-boosting-reach-8646916)). In April 2024 Instagram also changed its ranking to surface more content from smaller, original creators ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2024/04/30/instagram-is-updating-its-ranking-systems-to-surface-more-content-from-smaller-original-creators)).

Ignore the numbers that circulate around these signals. The claim that "sends are weighted 3–5x likes" comes from third-party data and Instagram has not confirmed it ([dev.to](https://dev.to/stklen/instagram-reels-algorithm-2026-the-3-official-ranking-factors-mme)). Buffer notes Mosseri said sends are only "slightly more important" ([Buffer](https://buffer.com/resources//instagram-algorithms/)). Keywords Everywhere warns that many "2026 algorithm update" articles are invented. Saves, comments and follows-from-Reel are often listed by vendors, but no 2025–2026 official statement ranks them against the top three.

Because every signal is measured **per reach**, the reaction of the first small test audience decides whether a Reel gets more distribution (inference). A Reel that 300 strangers finish, rewatch and forward beats one that 3,000 people scroll past. In practice, every Reel needs a specific moment someone would forward to a sibling, cousin or the family group. Every Reel should also be short enough that finishing it is easy.

## Originality is the series' biggest structural advantage

Instagram's 2026 crackdown on reposting helps an original animated series. On **30 April 2026**, Instagram extended its aggregator rules from Reels to photos and carousels. Accounts that mostly post unoriginal content become **ineligible for recommendations**, though followers still see their posts. Originality is judged per account over a **rolling 30 days**, Account Status shows where an account stands, and licensed content is exempt ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/30/instagram-restricts-reach-of-content-aggregators-in-new-crackdown/); [PetaPixel](https://petapixel.com/2026/04/30/new-instagram-policies-target-reposted-content/); [Tubefilter](https://tubefilter.com/2026/04/30/instagram-removes-algorithm-recommendations-repost-content-aggregator)). Mosseri said the target is "unoriginal content that [reposters] didn't enhance" ([Tubefilter](https://tubefilter.com/2026/04/30/instagram-removes-algorithm-recommendations-repost-content-aggregator)). Credited and Collab reposts are reportedly still allowed ([exchange4media](https://www.exchange4media.com/influence-zone-news/push-for-original-content-signals-bigger-reset-in-creator-economy-154828.html); single secondary source). The earlier version of the rule, reported in 2024, removed accounts from recommendations after **10 or more** unenhanced reposts in 30 days ([Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/instagrams-algorithm-overhaul-will-reward-original-content-and-penalize-aggregators-130018977.html)).

**Watermarks are the trap when cross-posting from YouTube.** Instagram has long avoided recommending Reels that carry other apps' logos. The TikTok-watermark penalty dates from 2021 ([Tubefilter](https://tubefilter.com/2021/02/10/instagram-reels-with-tiktok-watermark-less-discoverable/)). Earlier guidance also flagged blurry, text-covered or bordered videos ([ScreenRant](https://screenrant.com/instagram-reels-tab-tips-for-discovery-no-watermarks/)). Mosseri has said your **own** logo is fine ([Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-clarifies-that-including-your-own-logo-on-a-reel-is-ok/730852/)). No source found says Instagram detects or demotes un-watermarked content that was previously posted on YouTube. The researchers' inference is that a team posting **its own** Shorts natively, from a clean master file with no YouTube or Shorts interface and no CapCut or editor watermark, is posting original content. That conclusion follows from the watermark and authorship rules, not from any statement about YouTube Shorts.

**AI labels are less threatening than they sound.** On **31 August 2026** Instagram renamed its "AI creator" label to **"AI-generated profile"**. Accounts built around an AI-generated *person* that do not apply the label can lose Explore and Reels recommendations ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/31/instagram-puts-new-limits-on-undisclosed-ai-profiles/); [Instagram Creators](https://creators.instagram.com/blog/ai-generated-profile-label); [Engadget](https://engadget.com/2246914/instagram-will-demote-ai-generated-influencers-if-they-dont-clearly-label-their-account/)). Dadi, Chaman Bhoot and Chandni are openly cartoon characters, not synthetic humans presented as real, so the researchers infer the profile rule does not apply. The separate per-post **"AI info" label** is triggered by C2PA or SynthID metadata. Using AI for parts of production does not by itself require the profile label ([Metricool](https://metricool.com/instagrams-new-ai-label/), third-party). **No source found says the "AI info" post label reduces reach.** The TTS voices might carry such metadata, depending on the vendor (inference). The safe course is never to strip metadata to dodge a label, because the policy direction penalises non-disclosure, not disclosure. An optional line in the bio such as "AI voices" costs nothing (inference).

## Trial Reels are out of reach on day one; format choices carry the load

Trial Reels launched in December 2024. They show a Reel to non-followers first, keep it off the grid and away from followers, report results after about 24 hours, and let the creator publish the winner to followers afterwards ([Meta Newsroom](https://about.fb.com/news/2024/12/trial-reels-try-content-non-followers/); [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2024/12/10/instagram-rolls-out-trial-reels-that-arent-shown-to-a-creators-followers)). In mid-2025 Mosseri said they were open to **all public accounts with at least 1,000 followers** and that smaller accounts were under consideration ([BetaNews](https://betanews.com/2025/07/14/instagram-opens-up-trial-reels-feature-to-more-creators/); [Social Samosa](https://www.socialsamosa.com/news-2/instagram-introduces-wider-access-trial-reels-9493132)). Third-party guides quote the Help Center as **200+ followers for professional accounts**, but this is unverified ([Planoly](https://www.planoly.com/blog/how-to-post-a-trial-reel-on-instagram); [Hotmart](https://hotmart.com/en/blog/instagram-trial-reels)). Losing recommendation eligibility also removes access ([Planoly](https://www.planoly.com/blog/how-to-post-a-trial-reel-on-instagram)). **Conclusion: a 0-follower account almost certainly cannot use Trial Reels today.** Check for the "Trial" toggle in the composer anyway, and plan to use Trial Reels for hook testing once the account passes 200 followers.

Reels can now run **up to 3 minutes** and still be recommended to non-followers ([Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-will-recommend-longer-3-minute-reels/737913/); [Deccan Herald](https://www.deccanherald.com/technology/instagram-extends-reels-duration-from-90-seconds-to-3-minutes-3363553)). Third-party length data conflicts. Buffer's 2.7M-Reel dataset (published 12 Nov 2026) found **90–179 s Reels gained 7.4% reach** against each account's own baseline, while **180 s+ lost 49.3%** ([Buffer](https://buffer.com/library/instagram-reels-length)). Socialinsider found the highest engagement rate at **45–60 s** ([Socialinsider](https://socialinsider.io/blog/instagram-reels-length/)). A cross-platform study puts the sweet spot at 11–20 s ([Quso.ai](https://quso.ai/research/how-long-are-instagram-reels)). Buffer's averages describe accounts that already have followers. For a zero-follower account whose ranking depends on completion and rewatch, the researchers recommend **20–35 s loopable Reels** first, with 60–90 s mini-episodes tested once Trial Reels unlock (inference).

Hashtags were **capped at 5 per post in December 2025**. Mosseri says they help categorisation and search but do not increase reach, and generic tags like #reels or #explore do nothing ([Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/technology/news/story/no-more-hashtag-stuffing-instagram-limits-hashtag-usage-to-just-5-per-reel-and-post-507444-2025-12-19); [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-implements-new-limits-on-hashtag-use/808309/)). Captions matter more for search. Since **10 July 2025**, public posts from **professional accounts** (owners 18+) can be indexed by Google and Bing, with the caption as the main searchable text ([Tubefilter](https://www.tubefilter.com/2025/07/18/instagram-indexing-update-google-search-results/); [The Week](https://www.theweek.in/news/sci-tech/2025/07/12/social-media-instagram-posts-and-reels-will-now-pop-up-on-google-and-bing-searches-how-to-enable-disable-this-setting.amp.html)). That is one more reason to switch to a professional (Creator) account before the first post. Nothing found describes how well Instagram search handles Devanagari, so write the first caption line in **both Hindi and Roman Hinglish** ("दादी vs भूत | Hindi horror comedy cartoon | bhoot wali funny kahani") (inference).

Format specs are industry convention, because the official spec pages were blocked. Use 9:16 at 1080×1920. Keep essential text out of the top ~14% and bottom ~35% ([Buffer](https://buffer.com/resources/instagram-reels-instructions-and-ideas-for-small-businesses/)); another vendor says keep it in the middle ~60% ([Later](https://later.com/blog/instagram-reels-hacks/)). The profile grid has shown a **3:4 crop since January 2025**, so the cover frame must read in that crop ([Storyboard18](https://www.storyboard18.com/digital/instagram-extends-reels-to-three-minutes-and-updates-profile-layout-54043.htm)). Posting-time advice is weak. Third-party Indian guides cluster around **6–9 PM IST** ([WsCubeTech](https://www.wscubetech.com/blog/?p=15209); [Boss Wallah](https://bosswallah.com/blog/creator-hub/best-instagram-reels-posting-time-in-india-boost-views-engagement/)), but none discloses its method. India is a Reels-first market: Instagram tested a Reels-first home screen there in 2025 ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/09/29/instagram-is-testing-a-reels-first-ui-in-india-and-south-korea)), and a Meta/Ipsos survey found **92%** of Indian respondents prefer Reels for short video ([Storyboard18](https://storyboard18.com/brand-marketing/reels-drive-33-higher-engagement-2x-brand-recall-meta-study-80777.htm)).

## Recognition, not fear, makes Indian family humour travel

The strongest Indian evidence is about *why* grandmother content spreads. A viral clip of a 92-year-old choosing pizza over khichdi worked because it "reminded thousands of people of their own dadi or nani almost instantly" ([Asianet Newsable](https://newsable.asianetnews.com/india/92-year-old-grandma-choosing-pizza-over-khichdi-is-the-internet-new-favourite-moment-watch-viral-video-articleshow-w4s3lgg)). ThePrint describes comment sections under grandmother-banter Reels as "digital living rooms" ([ThePrint](https://theprint.in/ground-reports/indian-grandmothers-instagram-reels-videos-dating-apps-mental-health-sex-education/2983981/)). The "pro dadi" archetype is already native internet vocabulary, as in "Dadi toh pro hai" for a 90-plus woman driving an Alto ([Free Press Journal](https://www.freepressjournal.in/viral/dadi-toh-pro-hai-90-plus-jk-woman-spotted-skillfully-driving-alto-car-viral-video-stuns-internet)). The send trigger to design for is recognition. Dadi should behave like every viewer's dadi: offering the ghost chai and food, scolding him for being thin, asking when he will marry, declaring "आजकल के भूतों में दम नहीं" (inference). A family-safe tone with no gore and no adult jokes also makes the Reel forwardable into WhatsApp family groups, where admins tolerate content to keep the peace ([arXiv 2401.08091](https://www.arxiv.org/pdf/2401.08091)). No study measures Reels-to-WhatsApp sharing, so this route is inferred, not proven.

Indian genre precedents point the same way. Writer Amit Juneja, whose short horror story on Instagram passed 5M views, says "writing horror is 90% other genres and 10% horror" ([Free Press Journal](https://www.freepressjournal.in/lifestyle/amit-juneja-on-his-short-horror-stories-on-instagram-writing-horror-is-90-other-genres-and-10-horror)). Khooni Monday grew into a "10M+ community across platforms" on a recurring narrator voice and a **weekly ritual**, without showing a face for years ([FilmiBeat](https://filmibeat.com/bollywood/viral/2026/from-khooni-monday-to-horrorcon-divay-agarwal-on-building-indias-next-horror-entertainment-univers-518967.html); [Chartable](https://chartable.com/podcasts/the-social-nation-show/episodes/181111601-khooni-mondays-divay-agarwal-on-indias-horror-stories-hauntings-local-legends-halloween-special)). Majedar Kahani's animated **Ganji Chudail** went "from animated oddity to brand favourite", which shows a funny named chudail can become a sponsorable mascot ([afaqs](https://www.afaqs.com/news/influencer-marketing/ganjji-chudail-7092189); snippet only). Chandni is the obvious candidate. ThePrint's account of Instagram "AI romance comics" pulling 5–25 lakh views shows an appetite for **serialised, TV-serial-coded** storytelling ([ThePrint](https://theprint.in/opinion/viral-spiral/instagram-ai-romance-comics/2965265/)), so "Part 1 / Part 2" numbering suits this audience (inference). One evidence gap matters here. **No credible source gave Instagram growth data for any Hindi animated horror-comedy page**, and the desi meme-page success stories (Jeejaji at a reported 2.9M followers, for example) come from sponsored partner articles ([Tribune](https://www.tribuneindia.com/partner-exclusives/jeejaji-the-instagram-page-with-2-9m-followers-that-gets-the-drama-of-indian-life-just-right)).

### Voiceover craft favours character dialogue over narration

Mosseri says "the first three seconds of a reel are super important," and his first fix is to **put text on the video** ([Inman](https://www.inman.com/2025/03/07/head-of-instagram-reveals-secrets-to-social-media-success/)). A summary of his tips says **about half** of users scroll with sound off ([NowBam](https://nowbam.com/5-ways-to-increase-reach-on-instagram-from-the-ceo-himself/)). The widely quoted "85% on mute" figure is unsourced and should not be used. Instagram's own creation guide separates **text overlays** (key moments) from **captions** (all spoken audio) ([Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-shares-reels-creation-tips-in-new-guide/831551/)). A Hindi-voiced Reel therefore needs both: a bold hook overlay and full burned-in Devanagari captions. Indian trade press stresses that "mere translation is insufficient" and that local humour drives engagement ([exchange4media](https://www.exchange4media.com/influence-zone-news/one-brand-many-voices-the-shift-towards-localised-storytelling-156502.html)).

The rest is craft judgement, not sourced fact. Comedy lives in dialogue timing, so let the characters carry it: a theatrical, old-Hindustani Chaman Bhoot against a deadpan, unbothered Dadi, with at most one narrator line as the hook. Aim for about 2.5–3 Hindi words per second, and leave a deliberate beat of silence before Dadi's punchline. With TTS voices, that pause usually has to be added in the edit, because TTS rarely leaves comic beats on its own (inference). Use horror sound effects (creaking door, wind, a "booo" sting) to set up the scare. Land the Dadi beat with a comic sting or dead silence. Keep any music well under the voice. A recurring Dadi catchphrase published as **original audio** could become a reusable sound that others remix (inference). Instagram has historically told creators to favour original sound or its own library over audio ripped from other apps ([Fiverr](https://www.fiverr.com/resources/guides/digital-marketing/instagram-reels-tips)). No data compares the reach of original and trending audio.

**Meta AI Translations** can auto-dub Reels **Hindi ↔ English** (added October 2025) and later Bengali, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada and Marathi, for all public Instagram accounts ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/10/09/meta-adds-hindi-and-portuguese-support-for-its-ai-translation-feature-for-reels); [Meta Newsroom](https://about.fb.com/news/2025/11/instagram-empowers-creators-to-go-global-with-local-voice-translations-and-fonts/)). One outlet reports a limit of **two speakers per Reel** ([Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/technology/media/instagram-and-facebook-reels-get-hindi-voice-translations-with-meta-ai-1.500302428)). A three-character cartoon with distinct TTS voices may dub badly, so test it on one two-character Reel in week two rather than on launch day (inference).

## Five pitfalls can quietly kill reach

**Music copyright is the most expensive mistake.** Meta reportedly muted or removed more than 12M Indian Reels in 2024 for copyright, and Indian labels warned influencers in 2025 ([exchange4media](https://www.exchange4media.com/media-others-news/rs-900-cr-in-lost-royalties-the-hidden-cost-of-unlicensed-reel-audio-in-branded-content-149424.html); single trade source). In **Zee v. Nykaa** (filed April 2026, pending), Zee is seeking about ₹2 crore over 12 Reels. The argument is that Meta's library licence covers personal, non-commercial use only ([Intepat](https://www.intepat.com/blog/blog-zee-nykaa-music-copyright-instagram-india); [Mondaq](https://www.mondaq.com/india/licensing-syndication/1814464/commercial-use-of-music-on-social-media-analyzing-the-zee-entertainment-v-nykaa-copyright-infringement-dispute)). Library tracks can also be muted later when label deals change ([BOOM Live](https://www.boomlive.in/decode/instagram-music-muted-content-creators-reels-copyright-revenue-internet-music-company-19459)). A series meant to earn brand deals should use **only its own or royalty-free music and SFX**, never Bollywood horror themes or film dialogue.

**Reused content** is the second risk. Never post the same animation across several owned pages, never upload files with YouTube, Shorts or editor watermarks, and never pad the feed with clips from films or other creators. All of these move the account toward aggregator status ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/30/instagram-restricts-reach-of-content-aggregators-in-new-crackdown/)).

**Shock imagery** is the third. Instagram makes content that "may be considered upsetting, offensive, or sensitive" harder to find rather than removing it, and that includes violence ([Instagram Help](https://help.instagram.com/313829416281232); snippet). Graphic or shocking content is not recommended to non-followers ([TechRadar](https://www.techradar.com/news/instagram-wont-recommend-content-that-vaguely-violates-its-community-guidelines)). Repeat offenders can lose recommendation eligibility for a period, which **Account Status** shows ([Instagram Help](https://help.instagram.com/539126347315373)). No rule names cartoon horror. The researchers' inference is to keep the cover and first frame visibly comic: no blood, no realistic violence, no full-screen jump-scare face. Make the comedy legible from frame one so neither classifiers nor viewers read the Reel as disturbing.

**Sensitive cultural ground** is the fourth. No India-specific Instagram horror rule was found. Even so, ghosts and chudails touch religion, ritual and gender. Avoid mocking deities, real rituals, tantriks of a named community and caste markers. Avoid "witch" jokes that demean women, and give Chandni agency (inference). ThePrint has criticised AI-styled Indian comics for skewing toward lighter skin and glossy faces ([ThePrint](https://theprint.in/opinion/viral-spiral/instagram-ai-romance-comics/2965265/)). Check the character palette for colourism.

**Spam behaviour on a fresh account** is the fifth: mass-following, engagement-bait captions ("comment YES") and engagement pods. Spam is a non-recommendable category ([TechRadar](https://www.techradar.com/news/instagram-wont-recommend-content-that-vaguely-violates-its-community-guidelines)).

## Today's Reel, the first week, and the Shorts pipeline

The researchers' recommended first Reel follows a five-beat structure (all timings are craft estimates). From 0 to 2 s, cold-open on Chaman Bhoot mid-"बूऽऽऽ!" with the overlay "200 साल का भूत vs 1 दादी". Put no logo or title card first: an Indian creator quoted in exchange4media warns that "logo in first 3 seconds" rules hurt viewing ([exchange4media](https://www.exchange4media.com/digital-news/why-the-first-few-seconds-of-an-instagram-reel-can-make-or-break-an-influencer-ad-145586.html)). From 2 to 8 s, the ghost escalates with flickering lights and horror SFX, with a visual change every 1.5–2.5 s. From 8 to 18 s, Dadi turns it around, unimpressed: "चाय पियेगा?" From 18 to 28 s, the ghost gives up, gets put to work or sulks, followed by a one-second tease such as "…लेकिन अब आई है चाँदनी 👀". The last frame should cut naturally back into the opening "बूऽऽऽ" so the loop plays as one continuous gag. Other hook lines to rotate through the week: "जब भूत को पता चला कि घर में दादी है…", "POV: तुम भूत हो और तुम्हारी टक्कर दादी से है", "हर घर में एक दादी होती है जिससे भूत भी डरता है".

### First seven days on Instagram

The launch logic is to post one Reel a day, matching the existing daily YouTube Shorts, and to build a **pinned trio by day 3** so a stranger landing on the profile immediately understands the premise. Pinning up to three posts has been possible since 2022 ([Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/instagram-grid-pinning-now-available-180201676.html)). Grid reordering was reported on 8 June 2026 ([ABC7](https://abc7.com/post/meta-debuts-new-instagram-feature-allows-users-rearrange-posts-profile-grid/19262454/)), though TechCrunch reported the feature a year earlier ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/06/12/instagram-will-finally-let-you-rearrange-your-grid)), so the exact rollout date is uncertain. No source verifies "post 3–9 Reels at launch," and none gives an official posting frequency for new accounts. The cadence below is a judgement.

### Repurposing daily YouTube Shorts as Reels

Treat each Short as raw material, not a file to re-upload. Export from the project master, not from YouTube, so the file carries no Shorts interface, end-screen or third-party editor watermark. Re-check the first two seconds against Instagram's hook-plus-overlay pattern, and keep the captions inside Instagram's safe zones, which differ from YouTube's because Instagram's caption and button areas sit lower. Swap any YouTube Audio Library track that is not cleared for Instagram for your own SFX. Rewrite the caption for Instagram search in Hindi plus Hinglish, with at most five hashtags. Drop "subscribe" calls in favour of "भेजो उसको जो…" prompts that serve sends. Post once per Short on this one account only, with no duplicate pages. All of this is inference built on the watermark, originality and copyright rules above. **No source addresses YouTube-to-Instagram cross-posting directly**, and no data shows Instagram converting viewers into YouTube subscribers for animation channels ([IndianTelevision](https://indiantelevision.com/news-headline/kofluence-report-decodes-indias-booming-influence-economy/)). Measure the conversion with a link-in-bio click count rather than assuming it. Sharing Reels on to Facebook, whose audience skews older in India and suits a Dadi theme, is a free extra test. No 2025–2026 source quantified its effect on Instagram reach.

## Conclusion

The research shifts the real question away from "how do we game the algorithm on day one". For a zero-follower account, Instagram's own design already gives each Reel a test audience of strangers. What decides the outcome is whether those strangers forward it, and the Indian evidence says people forward grandmother content when they see their *own* dadi in it. So the horror in "दादी vs भूत" is the setup and Dadi's ordinariness is the payload. Every script should be judged by one question: who would a viewer send this to, and why? The same originality that YouTube's monetization review will scrutinise is a ranking asset on Instagram. The daily Shorts pipeline is therefore an advantage, as long as files stay watermark-free, music stays owned, and the episodes keep feeling different from one another.

Several things remain unknown and should be checked live once the blocked hosts are reachable or the account is old enough to show data. These include the current Trial Reels threshold (200 or 1,000 followers), whether TTS audio triggers an "AI info" label and whether that label affects reach, how well Instagram search handles Devanagari captions, and whether AI dubbing works with three distinct cartoon voices. The first week's own Insights, especially sends per reach, will soon be better evidence than any of the vendor benchmarks cited here.
