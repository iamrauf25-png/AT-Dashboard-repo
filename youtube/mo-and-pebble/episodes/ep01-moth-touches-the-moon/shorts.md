# Episode 1 — three Shorts (`/yt-shorts`)

Three moments from the script that still work without the rest of the episode. Each gets a
**new first line** (scored with `hookscore.py`), on-screen text that's different from the spoken
line, and a loop point.

Reframe for vertical (9:16): the porch is wide, so keep the bulb and Mo in the upper half and put
Pebble in the lower third. A tall frame suits this cast well because the light is above and the
rock is below.

---

### Short 1 — "Never Press the Moon's Button" · ~50s · post Fri, week 1
**Span:** Beat 3 → Payoff (1:40–2:45), sped up a little.
**New first line (Narrator) — 64 / WORKABLE:**
> "You should never press the one button on the moon. Mo is about to find out why, before Pebble can stop him."

**On-screen text, first 2s:** `THE MOON HAS A SWITCH`
**Loop:** it ends on the porch light clicking back on and Mo zooming at it. The first frame is Mo on the
bulb, so the loop joins up seamlessly.
**Lead with this one.** It has the strongest opening line and the biggest visual payoff (the blackout, then the real moon).

### Short 2 — "The Moon Is Made of Glass" · ~25s · post Mon, week 1
**Span:** Beat 1 (0:35–1:00).
**New first line (Narrator) — 55 / WORKABLE:**
> "You've failed at something 300 times? Mo has, and this is the first time he hit the moon. Nobody warned him why."

**On-screen text, first 2s:** `ATTEMPT #1`
**Loop:** it ends on "I knew it was fancy." Cut straight back to Mo launching at the bulb.

### Short 3 — "Why Moths Can't Fly Straight at Lights" · ~40s · post Wed, week 1
**Span:** Beat 2 (1:00–1:40).
**First line:** use Mo's own line, "Why can't I fly straight at it?! Every time I aim, the moon goes behind me!"
It's a real question in a character's voice, and that works better here than a narrator line.
**On-screen text, first 2s:** `WHY MOTHS CIRCLE LIGHTS`
**Loop:** it ends with Mo flying backwards into the door. Cut to the montage start.
**Only post this one if the citation in script.md checks out.** It's the Short that makes a factual claim.
