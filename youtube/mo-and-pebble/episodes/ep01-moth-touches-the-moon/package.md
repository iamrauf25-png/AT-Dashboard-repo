## Thumbnail brief

- **Composition**: Mo on the right third, hugging the glowing bulb with all six limbs, eyes huge
  and blissful. The bulb is the brightest thing in the frame. Pebble is small in the bottom-left corner,
  looking straight at the viewer with flat, unimpressed eyes. Pebble's look is the second joke.
- **Expression**: Mo shows pure joy; Pebble shows nothing at all. The contrast is the click.
- **Text**: "IT'S WARM?" in thick white letters with a dark outline, top-left, in the navy area. Two words.
- **Background**: flat deep navy (#0f1b3d-ish). Nothing else. The warm yellow glow against navy is
  what holds contrast at feed size. Don't add stars, because they become noise when small.
- **Test**: shrink it to 160 px wide. If you can't tell it's a moth on a light bulb, make Mo bigger.
- **Consistency**: every episode thumbnail uses the same layout (Mo right, Pebble bottom-left
  staring out, two words of text on navy) so the channel page reads as one series.
