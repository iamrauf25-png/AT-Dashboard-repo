# Episode 1 — description, tags, queries (`/yt-seo`)

A cartoon mostly gets found through the home feed and suggested videos, not search, so
packaging matters more than SEO here. The exception is episode 1, which happens to answer a real
question people search for ("why do moths fly at lights"). That's worth a little effort.

## The three queries this should win

1. `why do moths fly at lights`
2. `moth cartoon`
3. `funny moth cartoon for kids`

**Query test:** the title holds "moth" and "moon". The first two description lines hold "moth",
"cartoon" and "why moths fly at lights". Query 1 isn't in the title. If search traffic matters
after two weeks, switch to backup title #3 from package.md.

## Description (copy-paste)

```
Mo the moth has tried to reach the moon 300 times. Tonight it's ten feet away, on a porch. A short cartoon about a moth, a rock, and why moths fly at lights.

New Mo & Pebble episodes every other Saturday. Shorts all week.

The real science: moths keep their back to the brightest light. Outdoors that's the sky; near a lamp it makes them circle it.
Source: Fabian, S. T. et al. "Why flying insects gather at artificial light." Nature Communications (2024).

Mo is a moth who thinks every light is the moon. Pebble is a rock who has never moved and is always right. Mo & Pebble are short, original cartoons for kids and the grown-ups watching with them.

Next: Mo vs. the Window
```
