### COLD OPEN · 0:00–0:12
`[ON SCREEN: Black night. A porch. One glowing porch light. Mo hangs frozen in mid-air, an inch from the bulb, wings blurred. A big stamped counter in the corner reads "NIGHT 300".]`

**NARRATOR:** You have one second to stop Mo before he touches the moon. Pebble has had 300 nights, and he still hasn't moved.

`[ON SCREEN: Smash cut to Pebble on the bottom step. Blink.]`

**PEBBLE:** …No.

### THE TURN · 0:12–0:35
`[ON SCREEN: Rewind effect — tape squeal, the picture zips backwards. Caption: "EARLIER". Mo crashes onto the step beside Pebble.]`

**MO:** Pebble. PEBBLE. I found the moon. It's on a house.

**PEBBLE:** That's a light.

**MO:** It's the moon. And I'm going to touch it before you finish saying no.

**PEBBLE:** N—

`[ON SCREEN: Mo is already gone. A little puff of moth dust hangs where he was. Pebble's eyes slide up to follow.]`

### BEAT 1 — Attempt one · 0:35–1:00
`[ON SCREEN: Mo flies straight up at the bulb, full speed. TINK. He slides down the glass and drops onto the step, upside down.]`

**MO:** The moon is… hard.

**PEBBLE:** Glass.

**MO:** The moon is made of glass. *(sitting up, delighted)* I knew it was fancy.

### BEAT 2 — Attempts 2 to 299 · 1:00–1:40
`[ON SCREEN: Fast montage. Tally marks pile up on the porch wall behind Pebble. Mo with an acorn-cap helmet. Mo with a leaf parachute. Mo with a tiny map labelled "STEP ONE: MOON". Every time, he curves round the light in a spiral instead of hitting it.]`

**MO:** Why can't I fly straight at it?! Every time I aim, the moon goes behind me!

**PEBBLE:** You keep your back to the light.

**MO:** I do what?

**PEBBLE:** Moths keep their back to the brightest thing. Usually that's the sky. Tonight it's a bulb.

**MO:** *(thinks very hard)* …Then I'll fly backwards.

`[ON SCREEN: Mo flies backwards with great confidence, straight past the light, and into the front door. Thud. Tally mark 299.]`

### BEAT 3 — Attempt 300 · 1:40–2:20
`[ON SCREEN: Mo crawls up the wall instead of flying. Slowly. He reaches the bulb and climbs on top of it, arms spread, hugging it.]`

**MO:** I'm on the moon. I'm ON the moon! The moon is warm. The moon is… buzzing.

`[ON SCREEN: Close on the bulb: a soft electric hum.]`

**MO:** Pebble, I think the moon is hungry.

`[ON SCREEN: Mo spots the light switch on the wall below him.]`

**MO:** The moon has a… button.

**PEBBLE:** Don't.

`[ON SCREEN: Mo presses it with his whole body. CLICK. Total darkness. Three full seconds of silence, with only the crickets.]`

### PAYOFF · 2:20–2:45
`[ON SCREEN: The clouds slowly part. The REAL moon fills the sky: huge, white, silent. Mo's tiny silhouette in front of it.]`

**MO:** *(whispering)* Pebble. I broke the moon. And now there's a bigger one.

**PEBBLE:** That's the moon.

**MO:** Then what was that?

**PEBBLE:** A light.

**MO:** *(long beat)* …I'm going to touch it.

`[ON SCREEN: Mo launches toward the real moon and gets tinier and tinier — then CLICK, the porch light comes back on (someone inside flipped it). Mo instantly U-turns and zooms straight back to the bulb. TINK.]`

### CLOSE · 2:45–2:55
`[ON SCREEN: Pebble, alone in frame. The counter changes: NIGHT 301.]`

**PEBBLE:** Night 301.

`[ON SCREEN: End screen, 20 seconds. One element only: the "next episode" card for Mo vs. the Window, plus the subscribe circle. If ep 2 isn't up yet, use a playlist card instead.]`

## Fact check before you publish (required)

Pebble's line about moths keeping their back to the light comes from a 2024 study:
Fabian, S. T. et al., *"Why flying insects gather at artificial light"*, **Nature Communications** (2024).
It found that insects tilt their back toward the brightest light, which traps them circling
lamps. **Open the paper and confirm the citation before you put it in the description.** I'm
quoting it from memory and couldn't check it from here. If you can't confirm it, cut Pebble's
second line in Beat 2 and keep just "You keep your back to the light."
