# First month plan (`/yt-plan`)

**Assumption (change it if wrong):** you have about **10 hours a week**, alone, and you're new
to animation. Nothing exists yet.

At 10 hours a week, **one finished episode every week isn't realistic**. A 3-minute limited-animation
episode will probably take a beginner well over 10 hours. That's an estimate, not a measurement:
time episode 1 and resize this plan. So the plan runs on a **two-week cycle**:
an episode week, then a build week that also posts cheap Shorts. Three uploads a week, never seven.

## Before launch (weeks −3 to 0): build the stockpile

- Character sheets for Mo and Pebble (front, side, 6 Mo poses, Pebble's 4 mouth shapes).
- One porch background you'll reuse forever.
- **Finish episode 1 AND episode 2 before episode 1 goes live.** A one-episode buffer is what
  keeps a bad week from breaking the schedule.
- 4 "Pebble Says" Shorts (see below).
- Channel art and About text from `01-channel-bible.md`.

## The month

Upload day for episodes: **Saturday morning**, chosen because kids' viewing tends to peak at weekends.
That's a guess, not your data. After 4 episodes, check Studio → Analytics → Audience →
"When your viewers are on YouTube" and move it if needed. Keeping the same day every time
matters more than picking the perfect one.

### Episode ideas lined up after that (same engine, new light)
3. **Pebble's Birthday**: Mo throws a party with candles (new "moons"!) for a rock who doesn't know what a birthday is.
4. **Mo Gets a Job**: at a lighthouse. The biggest moon yet.
5. **The Firefly**: a "moon" that flies away. Mo meets his first rival.
6. **Phone Screen**: Mo finds a moon that lives in a pocket.

### "Pebble Says" (the cheap format)
15–25 seconds. One Mo line setting up a terrible plan, a long pause, Pebble's one-word answer, cut.
It reuses the same Pebble drawing every time, so it costs about an hour. It keeps the channel
posting during build weeks, and it trains viewers to know the joke.

## What this costs, honestly
Rough, unmeasured: ~1 episode per two weeks plus ~2–3 hours of Shorts a week. That's your 10
hours, with nothing to spare.

**If the week goes wrong, drop in this order:** (1) the second "Pebble Says", (2) the third
episode Short, (3) never the episode date. If an episode is late, post it late, but keep the next one on schedule.
