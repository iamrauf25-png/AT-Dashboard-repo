# दादी vs भूत: daily posting calendar

**Start: tomorrow, Wednesday 25 Nov 2026.** One Short **every day at 7:30 pm IST**, plus an
**original long episode every Sunday at 11:00 am IST**. Both times are starting guesses; after
two weeks, move them using Studio → Analytics → Audience → "When your viewers are on YouTube".

**Each week, about 20 minutes:** upload the week's files in one sitting and set each one to
**Visibility → Schedule** with the date and time below. YouTube then publishes daily on its own.
A new week appears here every Thursday afternoon (automation, see the bottom of this file).

Why it's laid out this way (from `reports/Monetizing Dadi vs Bhoot fast.md`):
- **Only long videos earn watch hours.** Shorts-feed watch time doesn't count toward the hours YouTube
  requires, so every Sunday is an original episode, never a compilation.
- **Endings are mixed.** YouTube's "inauthentic content" policy targets "a highly similar storyline
  template, with the same outcome". Dadi doesn't win every time here: the ghost gets hooked on a
  serial, gets a birthday gift, gets a job, gets engaged, and makes a Reel *with* Dadi.
- **Titles are mixed Hindi and Hinglish,** each unique, with the series name for search.
- **`long/compilation-01.mp4` is on hold.** Don't post it yet. A compilation that becomes the
  channel's top watch-time video goes straight into the YPP reviewer's sample. Re-use it in January as
  a "Season 1 recap" with new framing.

**Settings for every video:** *Not made for kids* · Category *Comedy* · Language *Hindi* ·
*Altered or synthetic content: No* (YouTube says stylized animation doesn't need the label and that
the label doesn't affect earnings; choose Yes if you're unsure). End every Short description with
`#DadiVsBhoot #bhoot #horrorcomedy #hindicartoon #shorts`. On each Short, under **Related video**, pick
the latest long episode. **Pin a comment**: `अगली बार भूत दादी को कैसे डराए? Comment करो 👇`
