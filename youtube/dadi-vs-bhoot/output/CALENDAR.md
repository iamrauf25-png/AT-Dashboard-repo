# दादी vs भूत: daily posting calendar

**Rhythm:** one Short **every day at 7:30 pm IST**, plus one long video **every Saturday at 11:00 am IST**.
(Both times are starting guesses. After two weeks, check Studio → Analytics → Audience → "When your
viewers are on YouTube" and move them.)

**How to keep it daily with about 20 minutes a week:** upload the whole week in one sitting and set each video to
**Visibility → Schedule** with its date and time below. YouTube then publishes one every day by itself.
A new week of videos appears in this folder every Thursday (see "Automation" at the bottom).

Example dates assume **Day 1 = Sunday 29 Nov 2026**. If you start on a different day, shift them all.

Shared settings for every video: *Not made for kids* · *Altered or synthetic content: Yes (synthetic
voices)* · Category *Comedy* · Language *Hindi* · Tags: `dadi vs bhoot, दादी vs भूत, bhoot cartoon,
horror comedy, hindi cartoon, bhoot wala cartoon, funny bhoot, animated shorts`.
Every Short's description ends with: `#DadiVsBhoot #bhoot #horrorcomedy #hindicartoon #shorts`

## Weeks 3+ (automation)

A scheduled task runs **every Thursday afternoon (IST)**. It writes and renders the next week's
7 Shorts plus 1 long video (a new episode or a compilation of the past week's Shorts), checks frames,
adds a new section to this file with the same columns, and pushes everything to the branch
`claude/wizardly-goodall-xpw90j`. You get a push notification when it's ready. Then do the 20-minute upload.
