# दादी vs भूत: Shorts titles and descriptions (copy-paste)

In posting order, matching `CALENDAR.md` (daily at 7:30 pm IST, starting Wed 25 Nov).

**How these are written** (from the two research reports):
- **Title:** a unique Hindi hook, one Hinglish search word, and "Dadi vs Bhoot", kept under about 70 characters. Every title is different, because YouTube's reviewers penalize copy-paste titles.
- **Description line 1** works as the hook: it shows in search and under the Short.
- **Line 2** has a comment question to drive comments.
- **Then** a line pointing to the latest full episode, and the hashtags. `#shorts` goes last.

**Same for every Short in YouTube Studio:**
- Audience: **No, it's not made for kids**
- Altered or synthetic content: **No** (stylized animation)
- Category: **Comedy**
- Language: **Hindi**
- **Related video:** the newest long episode (Ep 1 until 6 Dec, Ep 2 until 13 Dec, then Ep 3)
- **Tags** (optional; tags barely matter):
  ```
  dadi vs bhoot, दादी vs भूत, bhoot cartoon, hindi horror comedy, bhoot wala cartoon, hindi cartoon
  ```
