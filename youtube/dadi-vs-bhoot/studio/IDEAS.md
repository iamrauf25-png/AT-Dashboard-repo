## Rules from the monetization research (reports/Monetizing Dadi vs Bhoot fast.md)
- Every video needs a **distinct premise and a distinct ending**. At least a third of each week's Shorts must NOT end with "Dadi wins": the ghost wins, they team up, a third character wins, a twist, a sweet ending.
- Rotate formats: sight gags that work without sound, Chaman's point of view, two-part mini-arcs, festival/wedding specials, Chandni Chudail episodes.
- Sunday long videos are **original episodes only** (aim 6–10 min, longer over time). Compilations: at most one a month, only as a "season recap" with a new framing story.
- Humour for teens and adults (in-law politics, village satire, phones and bills). Never "for kids" wording, nursery music or child protagonists. Scares stay bloodless, especially in thumbnails and the first seconds.
- Titles unique per video: Hindi hook + one Hinglish keyword + "Dadi vs Bhoot".
